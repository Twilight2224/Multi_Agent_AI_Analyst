# Northstar Analytics — Operations Handbook

## Customer support

- Enterprise Priority 1 incidents receive a first response within **30 minutes**, 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
- Enterprise Priority 2 incidents receive a first response within **4 business hours**.
- Standard-plan Priority 1 incidents receive a first response within **4 business hours**.
- The Customer Success Director, **Nadia Karimova**, owns escalations that remain unresolved after two business days.

## Billing and refunds

- Customers may request a refund within **14 calendar days** of their first paid invoice.
- Refund requests must be submitted through the support portal with the invoice number.
- Annual-contract cancellations take effect at the end of the committed term.

## Product roadmap

- The CRM integration beta is planned for **15 September 2026**.
- The first beta supports HubSpot contacts and deals.
- Salesforce support is planned for the next roadmap cycle and has no committed release date.

## Churn-prevention playbook

- Customer Success must contact accounts marked at-risk within one business day.
- Missing integrations, price sensitivity, and slow support response are tracked churn themes.
- Do not claim that an unannounced feature has a release date.
